01 · Who I am

AI architect with product-delivery ownership

AI Architect and Module Lead with 8+ years of product engineering experience. I architect the enterprise AI layer at ARC Document Solutions: agentic orchestration, GraphRAG, long-term memory, a voice assistant endpoint and document / computer-vision AI, while leading a 12–15 member engineering team.

My work sits at the intersection of three things: enterprise full-stack delivery, applied AI, and team leadership. I care about the model, but just as much about the typed contracts, auth context, observability, error handling, and data boundaries around it.

Operating principle

AI should not be a demo layer bolted onto a product. It needs grounded data, scoped tools, guardrails, retries, graceful degradation and domain services underneath it, with the same rigor as any other production system.
